# msim — the millisecond Executive

An instruction-level, single-CPU simulator of the Apollo 11 LGC during P63
powered descent. One hundred microseconds per tick; one entry per
interpretive instruction, transcribed from the `Luminary099/` listing;
preemption only at the DANZIG boundary between instructions, exactly as
`INTERPRETER.agc` L74-L82 tests NEWJOB.

## What it answers

Key V16N68 (the DELTAH monitor) on a machine already losing 12.8-15% of its
cycles to the rendezvous-radar counter theft, and the Executive's pools
drain in about ten seconds: all eight core sets held, a VAC area still free
— **1202, NO CORE SETS** — twice, at the flight's own offsets, and never a
1201 in P63. Drop the monitor after six seconds (the crew's third use) and
nothing happens, exactly as on July 20, 1969.

## The two timelines

Generated by the simulator, committed under `timelines/`:

- [`timelines/p63-baseline.md`](timelines/p63-baseline.md) — radar locked,
  RR bug stealing, nobody touches the DSKY. 100 s clean on the knife edge.
- [`timelines/p63-monitor-1668.md`](timelines/p63-monitor-1668.md) — the
  same machine with Aldrin's keystrokes at the flight offsets. Two 1202s.

Regenerate with:

```bash
go run ./cmd/agc-timeline -out timelines -ms 100000
```

## Layout

- `engine.go` — the 1 ms tick: interrupt cadences (DAP 100 ms, T4RUPT
  120 ms, DOWNRUPT 20 ms), the waitlist, the RR CDU theft sweep, restarts
- `executive.go` — slot-true Executive: 8 core sets, 5 VAC areas, CHANJOB,
  EJSCAN, SETLOC, JOBSLEEP/JOBWAKE, full PRIORITY-word comparison
- `servicer.go` — the SERVICER instruction array: one P63 pass out of the
  real listing, ~370 entries, every one with its source line
- `jobs.go` — MONDO/MONREQ, MAKEPLAY (and its DSKY-blocked sleep), 1/GYRO,
  CHARIN, the landing-radar gates
- `scenario.go` — the two flight-anchored scenarios
- `render.go`, `cmd/agc-timeline/` — the timeline reports

Model, calibration, sources, and known deviations: [`RESEARCH.md`](RESEARCH.md).

Tests were written first (committed red), then the implementation; every
group covers a happy and an unhappy path.
